Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

JobProfit ("we", "our", "the app") is provided by tokoshie apps. Contact: tokoshie.apps@gmail.com.

Information you enter

The app lets you enter job names, customer names, financial figures, estimate and invoice details, payment information, notes, site photos, and receipt images. In the current version, these records and generated documents are stored locally on your device. We do not operate an account server that receives these records.

Subscriptions and purchases

Paid subscriptions are handled by Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes billing information; we do not receive your complete payment-card details. The app checks Apple's purchase entitlements to determine whether Pro features are available. Apple's privacy practices apply to information handled by Apple.

Photo access and sharing

You choose which photos to import using Apple's photo picker. Imported copies are saved within the app's local storage. When you choose to share an estimate, invoice, or other document, it is provided to the destination you select through the iOS share interface; the destination's own privacy policy may apply.

Data collection and analytics

The current app does not include a developer-operated analytics service, advertising SDK, or user-account backend. We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Apple's operating system and App Store may process technical and purchase-related information under Apple's own policies.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Records are retained on your device until you remove them in the app or remove the app and its data. Removing the app may permanently erase locally stored records. iOS device backups may contain app data according to your Apple backup settings. The current app does not provide cloud synchronization or a comprehensive in-app backup/export of all records.

Security

Data is stored within the iOS app sandbox and benefits from platform security controls. No storage method is completely risk-free. Protect your device and maintain appropriate backups.

Children

JobProfit is designed for business use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Your choices and rights

You can edit or remove many records within the app. For privacy questions or applicable rights requests, contact us. Because the current app stores business records locally rather than on our servers, we generally cannot access or delete those records remotely.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app's functionality or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated.
